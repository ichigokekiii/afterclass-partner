# What the wireframe boards show

Reference only. The judgment is in `explanation-assessment.md`.

Both boards are grayscale phone frames on a dark canvas. The wordmark on every frame is "After Class". The sample student is Jennie, 18, Ateneo De Manila University, with a pin labeled Diliman, Quezon City. A round photo sits where a portrait would go. None of the icons in the bottom bar have text labels.

## Board 1, discovery

File `board-discovery.png`. Three frames, left to right. Titles are printed above the phones.

### Main screen

A large card fills the phone. "Jennie" is at the top left of the card and "18" is at the top right. The middle of the card is the photo. A gray band at the bottom of the card reads "Ateneo De Manila University" and "Diliman, Quezon City" with a pin mark. The bottom bar holds three icons. A circle, a bow, and a rectangle.

### Main screen, first tab, on scroll

The printed title is cut off. It begins "Main Screen 1st tab(On Scroll/T". The card has become a profile sheet. A small round photo sits left of "Jennie, 18", the school name, and the Diliman pin. Three pager dots sit under that header. The first dot is filled. Five prompt tiles follow, in two rows.

- "A picture that describes you"
- "A moment that you can't forget"
- "Im Jennie because..."
- "Favorite thing in the world"
- "A meme I can't stop thinking about"

The same three bottom icons remain. No like control and no pass control are drawn on this frame.

### Card profile prompts, on tap

The profile sheet is dimmed. A dark panel covers the lower half. The panel title is "This Mirror Pic I have". A small round photo and the name "Jennie" sit at the bottom left of the panel. Under the phone, the board prints "Tap again to close".

## Board 2, meetup

File `board-meetup.png`. Four frames, left to right. No titles are printed above these phones. Each of the first three frames keeps Jennie's header, the three pager dots, and the same bottom bar. The header on these frames is inference that the step is still inside her profile. The board does not say that in a title.

### Month

The month label is "August" with "2026" at the right. Dates 1 through 31 are on the grid. No day is filled. Weekday letters are not printed on this frame.

### Day and times

August is still the month. The date 3 is filled black. Under the grid the label reads "Wed, Aug 3". Two full-width rows read "1:00 PM" and "5:00 PM".

August 3, 2026 is a Monday. October 15, 2026 is a Thursday, and August 3 is 73 days earlier. 73 mod 7 is 3, so the weekday is Monday. The frame's own label says Wednesday.

### Map

A schematic street map sits under the header. Four pin marks are on the streets. A round photo with the caption "Jennie" sits at the lower left of the map, on top of the map, separate from those four pins.

### Café sheet

This frame drops the bottom bar and the profile header. The top half is a street map of the España area. Readable labels on that map include Café Uno España, University of Santo Tomas (UST), and a pin callout that reads "Meet your match here!". The sheet below the map reads "Cafe Uno España", then "Friday, September 11", then "1:00 PM - 3:00 PM". Two storefront photos sit under the time. One photo shows an "uno" sign.

September 11, 2026 is a Friday. That weekday matches the calendar. The date does not match the selected day on the previous frame, which is August 3. The hours do not match the two rows on that frame. Those rows are 1:00 PM and 5:00 PM, with no end time. The sheet's window is two hours long, from 1:00 PM to 3:00 PM.

## What is not on either board

No like, pass, or swipe affordance. No match overlay. No second student's name. No chat thread. No safety check, arrival confirmation, shake, or trusted contact. No café login, no booking state, and no control a café would use to mark that the date happened.
