# What the reference pictures show

Reference only. The choice is in `explanation-direction.md`.

All four are polished marketing shots of other apps. None of them is After Class, and none of them shows a café confirming a student date.

## Medico

File `ref-medico.png`. A white phone on a magenta field. The wordmark is "Medico." A gradient card at the top reads "Fever Pack", with date, time, price, and a 4.0 rating. Under it, rows. Each row has a colored circle, a pill glyph, a name (Brevinor, Cerazette, Cilest, Dianette, Femodene), a dose, a price, and an empty circle at the trailing edge.

## Homedoctor

File `ref-homedoctor.png`. The same product family on a purple field. The center phone repeats the Fever Pack card and the drug rows, with prices and empty circles. The phone on the right keeps the rows and adds a block labeled dates and times. Time chips run from morning to evening, with an "ADD" label. A cart bubble sits on the last row.

## Coffee order

File `ref-coffee.png`. Three phones. A home screen greets "Esther Howard", searches "beverages or foods", and shows two drink cards with prices, then categories and a featured food photo. A detail screen shows one drink, a size slider, a quantity stepper, lorem ipsum, and a button "PLACE ORDER $17.4". A third phone is a green splash with a cup mark and the word "Coffee".

## Crypto

File `ref-crypto.png`. Three phones on a lilac field. A home screen has a black balance card with a large dollar figure, pastel green and purple holding cards, and a black pill tab bar. A middle screen is a bar chart. A third screen is a convert keypad with one black button, "Convert Coins".

## What all four share

A summary card first. A list or a set of cards second. One trailing control or one bottom button that finishes the job. White surfaces, large type for the number that matters, and a single saturated accent. Corners are round. Nothing on these shots is a month calendar, a dating profile, or a map.

## What none of them are

A café host. A booking with two student names. A state of happened or no-show. The After Class palette.
